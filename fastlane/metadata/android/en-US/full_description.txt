TG Monitor watches whether Telegram is reachable from your device.

• No Telegram account, phone number, or chat access
• MTProto handshake plus Telegram web endpoints
• Foreground service with status bar icon, quick settings tile, and home-screen widget
• Local seven-day history with time-weighted availability and CSV export
• Optional vibration on outage, offline, or recovery, with quiet hours
• Works offline for history; network probes stay on-device

Privacy first: the app does not send telemetry and does not use a Telegram login.
